# Spacefish

A three-phase moon run. Sneak the rim with the shovel, free the holdouts, repair the pad, then fly the swarm to Jupiter.

**Play:** [https://raven-flock.github.io/space-fish/](https://raven-flock.github.io/space-fish/)

## How to play

- **Start** plays a short opening on the rim, then the sneak. **Space** or **Enter** steps it. **Skip** jumps it. **WASD** or arrows move. On a phone, turn sideways and tap **Lock landscape**. If the browser refuses the lock, the wide view still fits. Drag the stick, or tap the ground to walk. **Fire**, **Use**, **Sneak**, and **Noise** are the buttons on the right.
- **Shift** sneaks. **Q** throws noise. On the rim you only have the shovel. After the cache, **Space** or click fires where you aim. **E** uses.
- **Esc** pauses. **Space** or **Enter** steps a cutscene. **Skip** jumps it.
- The south rim is cratered dust, a wrecked base on the horizon, and a quiet walk to the cache. No gun until then. After that, the laser fires along your aim and locks onto a fish already in that cone.
- On the pad, the generator, two turrets, the lab, and the pad are already wrecked. Pick one and tap the wreck to repair it (same scrap cost). Nothing new gets placed. Burn the nest, stand on the brass pad, and press **E**.
- In transit, grab the kits and change lanes. Jupiter is the eye at the end.
- Continue resumes the last phase on this device.
